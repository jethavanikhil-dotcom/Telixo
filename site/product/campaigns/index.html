(03)Stay on the right sideWhatsApp rules

Good habits for broadcasts.

WhatsApp protects its users. These habits keep your number in good standing.

  • Only message people who opted in

    Collect consent before you add someone to a campaign.

  • Use approved message templates

    WhatsApp requires approved templates for business-initiated messages.

  • Make it easy to stop

    Add a clear opt-out, and respect it.

  • Keep it relevant

    People who report or block your messages can get your number limited.
