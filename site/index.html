(07)FAQQuestions before a demo

Questions, answered.

Is Telixo an official WhatsApp Business API provider?

Answer to confirm with the team: how Telixo connects to the WhatsApp Business Platform, and whether it is, or works through, a Meta Business Partner.

Can several people use one WhatsApp number?

Yes. That is what the shared inbox is for: your whole team works from one WhatsApp Business number, and each chat can be assigned so the customer gets one clear reply.

Will broadcasts get my number blocked?

WhatsApp can limit or block numbers when people report or block their messages. To stay safe, send campaigns only to contacts who opted in, use approved message templates, and keep messages relevant.

Do I need coding skills?

No coding is needed to use the inbox, campaigns or contacts. Confirm how number connection works and whether the Telixo team helps with Meta setup.

How does calling work?

Telixo connects to Twilio, so your team can call customers from the same workspace where they chat. Confirm whether customers need their own Twilio account and how calls are billed.

Does Telixo work with Odoo?

Yes. Telixo syncs contacts with Odoo, so customer details stay the same in both tools.

How much does Telixo cost?

Telixo has flexible plans for businesses in India. See the pricing page for plans and features. Confirm whether Meta’s per-conversation fees are billed separately.

Is our customer data secure?

Roles let you control what each team member can see. Add only claims the team can prove: encryption, access controls, where data is stored, and DPDP Act compliance. More on the security page.
